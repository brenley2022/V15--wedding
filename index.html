Our story

Every great love story starts somewhere.

Ours started in October 2022.

At first, it wasn't the grand romantic gestures or fairy-tale moments that brought us together. It was conversation, laughter, and the discovery that we'd finally met someone who could keep up with us.

It didn't take long for us to discover we had something in common.

We're both incredibly competitive.

We both like to win. We both like to be right. And neither of us is known for backing down from a debate once we've decided we're right. Somewhere along the way, we learned that being right mattered a lot less than being together.

And somewhere between the friendly arguments, the laughter, and the occasional disagreement about who was actually right, something special happened.

We became each other's anchor.

Life has a way of throwing challenges at everyone, and we've certainly had our share. Through the highs and the lows, the exciting moments and the difficult ones, we've always found our way back to each other. No matter what was happening around us, we knew we had someone beside us who would listen, support, challenge, and love us.

As our relationship grew, so did our little family.

At the heart of it all is Aubrey.

She is our greatest adventure, our biggest source of pride, and the reason our home is so often filled with laughter. She keeps us busy, keeps us humble, and occasionally tests every ounce of patience we have. But the truth is, life simply wouldn't be the same without her. Watching the bond between the three of us grow has been one of the most rewarding parts of our journey together.

One of the things we love most about our life is sharing experiences. We rarely sit still for long. Whether it's discovering a new destination, trying something we've never done before, or simply finding somewhere new to explore, we've always believed that the best memories come from experiencing life together.

But what truly strengthened our relationship wasn't the adventures.

It was the trust.

Over the years we've shared things with each other that very few people know. We've seen one another at our best and at our worst. We've celebrated victories together and helped carry each other through difficult moments. We know each other's fears, weaknesses, insecurities and imperfections.

And somehow, knowing all of that has only made us love each other more.

Because real love isn't finding someone perfect.

It's finding someone who knows the whole story and chooses to stay.

Together we've built a beautiful home. Not just a house, but a place where we all feel safe, supported and loved. A place where we can be completely ourselves. A place where we can laugh, dream, grow, and build a future together.

Well, almost all of us feel safe.

Linley is still convinced that Luna is plotting against her.

Some stories need a villain.

To be fair, the evidence is mounting.

As we look ahead to the next chapter, we're incredibly grateful for everything that brought us here—the adventures we've shared, the challenges we've overcome, the family we've built, and the love that continues to grow every day.

Thank you for being part of our story.

We can't wait to celebrate this next chapter with you.

Brendan, Linley & Aubrey ❤️
